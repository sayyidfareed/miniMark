miniMark Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

miniMark is a Markdown editor for the Mac. It works on files on your Mac and does not collect your data. This page says what the app does and does not do.

What we collect

Nothing. miniMark has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your documents or any information about you to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your Markdown files are opened, edited and saved on your Mac, where you choose.
  • Settings such as the Source or Preview default and the light or dark appearance are stored on your Mac.
  • If you allow miniMark to read a folder so that images next to your document can show in the preview, macOS stores that permission on your Mac.
  • Export, print and Save as PDF happen on your Mac.

Pictures and links on the web

The preview is drawn with Apple’s WebKit. If a document refers to a picture that lives on a website, the preview asks that website for the picture, as any browser would, and that website can see the request. Links you click open in your default browser. miniMark itself sends nothing.

Code that comes with the app

The diagram and math features use open-source code that ships inside the app and runs on your Mac. See the notices file in the project for the list. miniMark does not use any of it to send data anywhere.

Purchases

If you buy miniMark from the Mac App Store, Apple handles the purchase under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We receive sales totals from Apple, not personal details about buyers.

Children

miniMark collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact
