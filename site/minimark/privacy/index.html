miniMark Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

miniMark collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and nothing you write is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your files

Your files stay on your Mac. miniMark opens and saves them where you choose, and its settings stay on your Mac too.

Pictures from websites

If a document shows a picture from a website, your Mac loads it from that site, the way a web browser would, and that site can see the request. miniMark itself sends nothing.

Buying miniMark

The Mac App Store handles your purchase under Apple’s terms. We see sales totals, not who bought.

Children

We collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date will be posted here.
